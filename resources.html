Ages 9–14 Storytelling

Junior Story Structure: The Hero's 5-Box Method

Universal framework for narrative school essays: Character → Challenge → Attempt → Climax → Resolution.

Box 1 (Hook): Introduce hero and an unexpected everyday event.
Box 2 (Conflict): The sudden problem or mystery arises.
Box 3 (Twist): The hero's first attempt fails; an ally offers a hint.
Box 4 (Climax): Facing the major challenge with bravery and smarts.
Box 5 (Lesson): The resolution, personal growth, and new perspective.
